Privacy Policy

🔒 GDPR & PIPEDA Compliant

Dom's Barber Lounge ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our booking services. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you book an appointment, contact us, or sign up for our newsletter, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Appointment preferences and service history
  • Messages and communications you send us
  • Review submissions (name and review text)
  • Newsletter sign-up details

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location (city/region)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Confirm, manage, and remind you of appointments
  • Send you service-related communications
  • Respond to your enquiries and messages
  • Send promotional emails (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and maintain security

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers — such as email delivery services, SMS providers, and booking software, who act on our behalf
  • Analytics providers — such as Google Analytics, to understand website usage (data is anonymized)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect our rights

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may only use it for the purposes we specify.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the website to function (cookie consent preferences, booking session data)
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Marketing cookies — Used to deliver relevant promotions (only with consent)

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time via our Cookie Policy page or your browser settings. See our full Cookie Policy for details.

5. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent — Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint — File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@domsbarberlounge.ca.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Appointment records are typically kept for 2 years. Marketing consent records are kept until you withdraw consent. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our website after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
