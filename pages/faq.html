Booking & Appointments

You can book in three ways:

1. Online — Use our booking page to pick your service, date, and time. Available 24/7.
2. Phone — Call 613-725-2222 during business hours.
3. Walk-in — Subject to availability. We recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred slot.

We recommend booking 2–3 days in advance for weekday appointments, and 5–7 days ahead for Saturdays and peak times. Same-day appointments are sometimes available — call us or check online to see current availability.

Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome based on availability. If we have an open chair, we'll take you right away. For a guaranteed time slot, especially on weekends, we strongly recommend booking online in advance.

We ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. You can:

  • Call us at 613-725-2222
