MEET YOUR BARBER

Dom — Master Barber

Dom discovered his passion for barbering at a young age — drawn to the way a great haircut could change how someone felt about themselves. What started as a calling became a craft, and what became a craft became Dom's Barber Lounge.

After completing his professional barber training and earning his Ontario barbering license, Dom spent years refining his technique across Toronto before opening his own lounge at 60 Lyndale Ave in the heart of East York. His philosophy is simple: every client deserves the same level of care, precision, and attention — whether it's their first cut or their fiftieth.

Dom specializes in skin fades, beard sculpting, and classic men's cuts, staying current with the latest techniques through ongoing education and industry workshops. He's served over 5,000 clients across Toronto and has built a loyal following because of one thing: consistency. You'll get the same excellent result every single time.
